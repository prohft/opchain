Late to the Move

Indicator Overload

Too many indicators create noise instead of clarity. You need cleaner decision-making, not more clutter.

Sudden reversals, gamma spikes, short-covering — options move fast. Fear of being wrong leads to early exits or holding losers too long.

Workflow

How OpChain Works

A simple, structured reading flow for fast-moving intraday sessions.

1

Check the Main Scenario

Start with the scenario row: Strong Buy, Buy, Neutral Buy, Neutral, Neutral Sell, Sell, or Strong Sell. This gives you immediate directional context.

2

Note the Strength Percentage

Higher percentages indicate broader alignment across monitored strikes. Lower values suggest mixed structure or the need for patience.

3

Verify Spot-Futures Context

Confirm whether spot and futures indicators support the same directional side. Alignment increases conviction.

4

Scan the Strike Table

Review call-side (CE) and put-side (PE) directional markers. Check if the structure is broadly aligned or still mixed.

5

Make Your Decision

Align OpChain's reading with your own price-action view. Enter when both agree, or exercise patience when structure is unclear.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is OpChain access limited?

We believe in maintaining the exclusivity and effectiveness of OpChain by limiting access to serious traders only. When too many market participants use the same tool, its edge diminishes. Early adopters get priority access and lifetime updates.

Is OpChain suitable for beginners?

Yes, but it's most useful for traders who already understand basic chart structure and options trading. The 5-minute timeframe with slow mode is recommended for beginners.

Which timeframe should I use?

The 5-minute timeframe is the most reliable default for most users. Switch to 1-minute during fast sessions, large gaps, or when you need earlier reversal signals.

How can I get support?

Currently, support is available through Telegram, X (Twitter) DM, and email. We provide personalized assistance to all OpChain users. Phone support will be added once we build our support team.

Does OpChain work for replay-based learning?

Absolutely. It's designed around real intraday workflow and is also useful for replay sessions, training content, and educational demos.

Can I use OpChain on mobile?

OpChain works on TradingView, which supports mobile viewing. However, the dashboard is optimized for desktop screens where you can see all strike details clearly.

What markets does OpChain support?

OpChain supports both Indian markets (NSE, MCX) and US markets (NASDAQ, OPRA). Configure your market mode in the settings panel.
