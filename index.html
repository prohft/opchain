Scenario language

A quick vocabulary for market conditions.

Bullish & transition
Strong Buy · Buy · Neutral Buy
• Strong Buy: broad call/put + spot/futures alignment favouring upside continuation.
• Buy: bullish bias with slightly less alignment – good for intraday continuation and scaling.
• Neutral Buy: improvement from bearish or neutral structure, often an early sign to watch for long setups.
Neutral & bearish
Neutral · Neutral Sell · Sell · Strong Sell
• Neutral: balanced or mixed; better for reduced size or range-based structures.
• Neutral Sell: pressure shifting bearish – useful for trimming longs and preparing shorts.
• Sell / Strong Sell: downside alignment building; time to protect long carry or express a defensive bias.

Practical playbook

Timeframes, flips and carry-forward rules.

Session handling
Choosing timeframe & logic mode
• 5-minute + Slow mode: default for most sessions – cleaner structure and easier for beginners.
• 1-minute: use during big gap opens or fast volatility spikes to see how scenarios rebuild.
• Fast mode: helpful in choppy markets, but requires tighter risk management because of added noise.
• Keep higher timeframes (15m / 1h) in mind for context; OpChain works on the execution timeframe.
Carry-forward behaviour
Previous day vs next day
• Gradual flips from Strong Sell → Sell → Neutral → Buy → Strong Buy into the close often support next-day buying, even after modest gap-downs, if the environment is stable.
• Sudden flips (e.g., Strong Sell close → gap-up open) demand caution – intraday upside may be tradable, but overnight bullish carry is more fragile.
• Treat such sudden changes as potential traps unless structure later rebuilds gradually in the new direction.

Risk & position sizing

Let size follow scenario quality.

Sizing logic
Adjust exposure with structure
• During corrections with Strong Sell / Sell, focus on protecting capital and expressing cautious shorts.
• When structure improves to Neutral then Neutral Buy, close shorts and begin building long exposure gradually.
• Increase size only when scenarios advance to Buy or Strong Buy and price action supports the move.
• Avoid max size in mixed or rapidly flipping conditions; keep trades smaller and shorter.
Confirmation layer
OpChain + your own analysis
• Start with your existing process: price action, liquidity zones, higher timeframe trend and event calendar.
• Use OpChain to confirm or challenge your idea – alignment should raise conviction, conflict should slow you down.
• Keep a journal: note which scenario transitions work best on your symbols and when to ignore weaker readings.
