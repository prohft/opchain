How to Use

Trade with the right playbook

Regime is a context tool, not an entry signal generator. Use it to decide which strategy style fits the current market before you act.

1

Check the current regime reading

Glance at the oscillator and zone coloring to see whether the market is trending, ranging, or transitioning between the two.

2

Match your strategy to the regime

Favor trend-following setups in trending regimes and mean-reversion setups in ranging regimes. Stay cautious during transitional readings.

3

Confirm with price action

Use the regime read alongside support/resistance, volume, and structure — not as a standalone trigger.

4

Manage risk independently

Regime highlights market conditions; you still define stop-loss, target, and position size based on your own plan.

Important

What Regime is NOT

Understand its boundaries before adding it to your workflow.

⚠️

Not a Standalone System

Regime does not provide entry, exit, or risk management rules. It classifies market conditions for you to act on within your own strategy.

🔄

Not Predictive

Readings reflect current and recent conditions. They do not guarantee future price behavior or regime persistence.

📉

Not Immune to Whipsaws

Rapid regime shifts can occur around news events or low-liquidity periods. Always check higher-timeframe context.

🎯

Not Financial Advice

This is an analytical tool for educational and informational use. All trading decisions and their outcomes are your responsibility.

FAQ

Common Questions

What does Regime actually do?

Regime blends multiple internal factors into a single composite oscillator that classifies current market conditions as trending, ranging, or transitional.

Do I need OpChain to use Regime?

No. Regime is a standalone indicator, but it's included as part of the full ProHFT indicator suite for OpChain subscribers.

Can I use Regime for intraday trading?

Yes. Regime works on all timeframes, including 1-minute and 5-minute charts. Always validate with price action before entry.

How is this different from ADX or other trend indicators?

Regime combines several underlying factors — not just trend strength — into one composite read, designed for discretionary use alongside your strategy rather than as a single mechanical input.

Is the core logic disclosed?

No. The internal calculation framework is proprietary and not shared publicly. The indicator is provided as-is for use within TradingView.

What markets does Regime support?

Any market with TradingView data: stocks, indices, forex, crypto, commodities, futures. The logic is market-agnostic.
