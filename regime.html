Why It Works

Why one factor alone isn't enough

The reasoning behind the read

Trend strength alone can stay elevated during a slow grind that never actually trends. Momentum alone can spike on noise that fades within a few bars. Judged in isolation, each factor produces false reads often enough to erode confidence in any single indicator.

Regime doesn't rely on one measure. It requires multiple independent factors to agree before classifying a condition as trending or ranging, and flags the space in between as transitional — because that ambiguous middle ground is exactly where most bad trades get taken. The result is a read that's slower to flip but far less prone to whipsawing you between conflicting signals.

We don't publish the exact internal weighting or formula — that's what keeps the tool consistent and resistant to over-fitting by every trader tweaking it to their last trade. What you get is the output: a clean, actionable regime read you can build a repeatable process around.

Common Questions

What does Regime actually do?

Regime blends multiple internal factors into a single composite oscillator that classifies current market conditions as trending, ranging, or transitional.

Do I need OpChain to use it?

No. Regime is a fully standalone indicator with its own access — you don't need OpChain or any other ProHFT product to use it.

Can I use Regime for intraday trading?

Yes. Regime works on all timeframes, including 1-minute and 5-minute charts. Always validate with price action before entry.

How is this different from ADX or other trend indicators?

Regime combines several underlying factors — not just trend strength — into one composite read, designed for discretionary use alongside your strategy rather than as a single mechanical input.

Is the core logic disclosed?

No. The internal calculation framework is proprietary and not shared publicly. The indicator is provided as-is for use within TradingView.

Does it repaint or lag?

Regime updates on confirmed bar data for its classification logic. Like any composite read, brief transitional readings can occur around fast-moving conditions — this is disclosed, not hidden.

What markets does Regime support?

Any market with TradingView data: stocks, indices, forex, crypto, commodities, futures. The logic is market-agnostic.
