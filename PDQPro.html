Why It Works

Why price and volume delta, together

The reasoning behind the read

Price tells you where the market moved. Volume delta tells you how much conviction was behind that move. Read separately, either one can mislead you — a strong-looking candle on weak participation, or heavy volume on a session that goes nowhere.

PDQ-Pro scores both independently on the same session structure and combines them into one read. When price and delta agree, that's confluence worth acting on. When they diverge, that's flagged too — often the earliest hint that a move lacks the conviction to continue. The reference levels are then built from the same session structure, so they're grounded in how the market actually traded, not an arbitrary percentage or fixed distance.

The exact scoring mechanics and thresholds stay internal — that's what keeps the output consistent across symbols and sessions instead of being re-tuned into overfitting. What you see is the finished read: a bias, a confidence grade, and levels you can act on.
