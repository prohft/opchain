Overview

A focused tool for studying exhaustion and reversal context.

OSOB is designed for traders who want potential reversal zones highlighted only when the setup quality is worth a closer look. It works best as a timing and attention tool inside a broader process that already includes structure, levels, trend state, and execution discipline.

Selective output

Instead of flooding the chart with constant extremes, the page messaging positions OSOB as a more measured signal overlay.

Equal-weight signal paths

Where the script may currently expose separate trigger families, this page presents them neutrally as alternate confirmation paths of equal importance.

Workflow-friendly design

Display controls, label visibility, and alert compatibility make it practical for traders who review setups with context instead of reacting blindly.

Best-use framework

Use the label as timing support, not as the whole thesis.

The strongest OSOB usage comes from placing the signal inside an existing market read. A disciplined process keeps the label useful without asking it to do more than it was built for.

1

Start with context

Review higher-timeframe trend, range structure, or expansion conditions before taking the chart label seriously.

2

Map important levels

Use prior highs and lows, session zones, supply-demand areas, or liquidity locations as the decision frame.

3

Wait for location + signal

Treat the OS or OB label as a timing alert that tells you where attention may now be justified.

4

Define execution

Look for price behavior, define invalidation, and manage the setup with risk controls already planned in advance.

Where it fits well

  • Range conditions where exhaustion zones matter.
  • Pullback or fade setups when the broader context supports mean reversion.
  • Alert-driven monitoring for discretionary review.
  • Combining price action with support, resistance, and higher-timeframe bias.

How not to use it

  • Do not treat every signal as an automatic trade entry.
  • Do not assume every OB label means a top or every OS label means a bottom.
  • Do not rely on the indicator without invalidation, sizing rules, and market context.
  • Do not market or interpret it as a guaranteed reversal detector.
Trust and framing

Built to improve focus, not replace judgment.

ProHFT OSOB should be positioned as a structured analytical aid that helps traders study potential reversal zones more systematically. The messaging stays intentionally honest: the tool supports review, attention, and confirmation, while the trader still owns context, execution, and risk.

FAQ

Clear expectations for serious traders.

What does the indicator do?

It highlights potential overbought and oversold reversal zones directly on the chart through a selective, confirmation-based presentation style.

Is this a buy or sell system?

No. It is a chart signal and analysis aid, not a complete trading system or a substitute for market context.

Does it predict tops and bottoms?

No. It highlights areas where reversal conditions may deserve attention, but it does not promise a turn or a profitable trade.

Can it be used on any timeframe?

Yes, it is intended for use across timeframes, though how useful it feels will depend on the market, volatility regime, and the trader's process.

Are the two visible signal groups different in importance?

No. This page treats them as equal-weight signal types that arise from different confirmation paths, without ranking one above the other.

Is the internal logic shown here?

No. The page keeps the indicator logic private and explains the tool only at a trader-facing conceptual level.
